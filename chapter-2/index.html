THE CROWN

Duty Calls: A Chronicle of Queen Elizabeth II's Reign

Key Information

  • Network: Netflix
  • Creator: Peter Morgan
  • Genre: Historical Drama

The Plot

The Crown traces the life of Queen Elizabeth II from her wedding in 1947 through to the 21st century. It explores the political rivalries and personal intrigues across several decades of her iconic reign.

Cast & Creative Team

  • Claire Foy / Olivia Colman / Imelda Staunton: Queen Elizabeth II
  • Matt Smith / Tobias Menzies / Jonathan Pryce: Prince Philip
  • Vanessa Kirby / Helena Bonham Carter: Princess Margaret
