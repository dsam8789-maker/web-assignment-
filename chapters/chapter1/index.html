Stuffed Grape Leaves (Warak Enab)

A Mediterranean and Middle Eastern delicacy featuring tender grape leaves stuffed with a flavorful mixture of rice, herbs, and spices.

Ingredients

  • 500g Grape leaves
  • 2 cups Egyptian or short-grain rice
  • 1 bunch of fresh parsley
  • 2 medium tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1/2 cup Pomegranate molasses
  • 1/2 cup Extra virgin olive oil

Method

  1. Wash and drain the grape leaves, then blanch briefly in boiling water to soften them.
  2. Rinse the rice and mix it with finely chopped parsley, diced tomatoes, chopped onion, olive oil, and salt.
  3. Place a spoonful of the rice mixture on each leaf, fold the sides, and roll tightly.
  4. Arrange the rolls in a pot, seam-side down, and cover with extra grape leaves.
  5. Pour enough water and pomegranate molasses to cover the rolls, then simmer gently for 45-60 minutes.
  6. Let the stuffed leaves cool slightly before serving with yogurt or lemon wedges.
